beigeboxes: MartyPC IBM PC/XT core (public/emulators/martypc/)
================================================================

martypc_web.js, martypc_web_bg.wasm, martypc.js
    The wrapper (emulators/martypc-web in the beigeboxes repo) is
    Copyright (C) 2026 Michał Jaskólski, GPL-3.0-or-later.

    It statically links MartyPC's emulator core (marty_core, marty_common),
    https://github.com/dbalsom/martypc at 05c0d088e84ad6bbfac9b3f0d051e7eadefd9f44,
    Copyright 2022-2026 Daniel Balsom, MIT licence: see LICENSE-MartyPC-MIT.txt.
    And fluxfox (https://github.com/dbalsom/fluxfox, MIT) plus other MIT/Apache-2.0
    Rust crates (wasm-bindgen, js-sys, crossbeam, serde, ...).

Character generator bitmaps inside martypc_web_bg.wasm
    The MDA 8x14, CGA 8x8 and Tandy 1000 8x9 glyph bitmaps come from
    The Ultimate Oldschool PC Font Pack v2.2, (c) 2016-2020 VileR,
    https://int10h.org/oldschool-pc-fonts/ , licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
    (LICENSE-fonts-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). Fonts used: Bm437_IBM_MDA, Bm437_IBM_CGA,
    Bm437_Tandy1K-I_200L, converted from .FON to a 256-glyph row-major bitmap
    (only the leftmost 8 columns of the 9-wide MDA cells are kept; the MDA's
    9th column is generated by the emulated hardware).

roms/GLABIOS_0.4.2_8X.ROM
    GLaBIOS 0.4.2, IBM PC/XT (5160) 8088 build, (c) 640KB, GPL-3.0:
    roms/LICENSE-GLaBIOS-GPL-3.0.txt; source https://github.com/640-KB/GLaBIOS
    (tag v0.4.2). Unmodified release binary.

roms/ide_xtl.bin
    XTIDE Universal BIOS r631 (2025-02-01), XT build configured for an XT-IDE rev 2
    at 0x300, (C) 2009-2010 Tomi Tilli, 2011-2025 XTIDE Universal BIOS Team,
    GPL-2.0-or-later: roms/LICENSE-XUB-GPL-2.0.txt; source
    https://www.xtideuniversalbios.org/ (Subversion trunk r631). Binary as shipped
    by MartyPC (install/media/roms/XUB/ide_xtl.bin at the revision above).

No IBM ROMs (BIOS, BASIC, or character generator) are distributed here.
