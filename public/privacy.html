What tech support keeps

beigeboxes's tech support (the flyer on the cork board) is answered through ALIAS, an anonymous support service. This is everything it keeps, and for how long.

What we keep when you ask for help

When you call in a problem from beigeboxes, we keep:

  • what you wrote;
  • the picture of the emulator's screen, if you chose to send one. You can black out parts of it before sending it. Pictures lose their hidden details when they reach us;
  • the details beigeboxes adds about the emulated machine: the case, monitor and memory, the names of the disks in its drives, whether it was on, the network and modem settings and the screen size, and your browser's name and version. You see all of it before you send, and you can take out the browser's;
  • the version of beigeboxes;
  • our answers.

Who you are to us

Nobody, by name. The first time beigeboxes talks to the support service, the service gives your browser an anonymous id and a secret. They aren't made from anything about you, your device or any account. The id is beigeboxes's alone: if you use another product with the same support service, it gets a different id, and the two can't be linked. We don't ask for, and don't keep, your name, email, phone number or account — unless you choose to give an email (below).

How long we keep it

  • A call: 365 days after it was resolved or closed.
  • Everything of your id: 730 days after beigeboxes last talked to the support service.

Both are deleted automatically.

Your copy, and erasing it

At the bottom of the tech support pad you can download everything we keep about your id, as a file, or erase all of it at once. Your id, its calls and pictures, and any email you gave are deleted, for good, at once.

Who sees it

Only the people who answer beigeboxes's support, signed in with their organisation's account, see it. Everything they do (answering, blocking, erasing) is logged. We use your calls only to help you and to fix beigeboxes. We never sell them or use them for advertising.

An email, only if you choose to give one

You may give us an email address so we can write to you about your calls. Support works the same without it. If you give one, we keep the address and when you gave it, and use it only to answer your calls to beigeboxes — never for news, offers or anything else. It isn't shared with any other product.

With an email, you're no longer anonymous to us: the people who answer can see it, and every time one of them opens it, that's logged. We don't check it: we send no mail to confirm it, and it's shown to support as unverified.

It's deleted when you erase your data, and when your id is deleted after 730 days without use. We keep it only because you agreed by giving it to us (consent: GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).
